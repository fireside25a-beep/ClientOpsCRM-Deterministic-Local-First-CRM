\section{ClientOpsCRM 2.0.0 Glossary}\label{clientopscrm-200-glossary}

\textbf{Account} --- Organization/company record. May own many contacts
and deals.

\textbf{Activity} --- Business-facing event in an entity timeline, such
as creation, qualification, conversion, stage move or closure.

\textbf{Actor} --- Operator/automation identity attached to audit
mutations through the CRM connection context.

\textbf{Audit log} --- Append-only database record of mutations with
actor and JSON before/after evidence where applicable.

\textbf{ClientOps Relay} --- Existing 1.2.1 deterministic
intake/automation subsystem retained in the combined release.

\textbf{ClientOpsCRM} --- Compiled operator-side CRM introduced in
version 2.0.0.

\textbf{Contact} --- Person record, optionally associated with an
account.

\textbf{Conversion} --- Transaction that transforms a qualified lead
into durable account/contact/deal links and marks the lead converted.

\textbf{Custom value} --- Operator-defined key/value attached to an
account, contact, lead or deal; key syntax is constrained.

\textbf{Deal / opportunity} --- Qualified commercial pursuit that
belongs to a pipeline and stage until won/lost.

\textbf{Dedupe signal} --- Normalized identity collision presented for
review; not every signal is automatically merged.

\textbf{Doctor} --- Executable database/invariant health check.

\textbf{Expected close} --- Validated calendar date associated with a
deal.

\textbf{FTS5} --- SQLite full-text-search extension used by
\texttt{search}.

\textbf{Lead} --- Pre-opportunity record that may be new, qualified,
disqualified or converted.

\textbf{Minor units} --- Integer representation of money at two decimal
places (e.g. EUR 12.34 → 1234) used to avoid binary floating-point
storage.

\textbf{Normalized identity} --- Canonical form used for
uniqueness/matching, such as lowercase email/domain or digits-only
phone.

\textbf{Owner} --- Business ownership label stored on the relevant CRM
record.

\textbf{Pipeline} --- Ordered sales process containing stages.

\textbf{Probability} --- Integer 0..100 attached to a stage and
synchronized into a deal for deterministic weighted pipeline reporting.

\textbf{Relay ticket ID} --- Stable UUID from Relay used as the import
identity for a CRM lead.

\textbf{Stage} --- Ordered pipeline state with name, probability and
kind (\texttt{open}, \texttt{won}, \texttt{lost}).

\textbf{Task} --- Follow-up work item attached to one core CRM entity.

\textbf{Timeline} --- Ordered activity view for business history;
distinct from mutation audit.

\textbf{Transactional} --- A multi-step operation that commits
atomically or rolls back as a unit.

\textbf{Weighted forecast} --- Sum of amount\_minor × probability / 100
across open deals; an inspectable arithmetic summary, not a learned
predictor.
