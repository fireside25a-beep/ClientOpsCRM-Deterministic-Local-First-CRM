# Delivery contract

Deliver one master ZIP containing tidy inner ZIPs:

- SOURCE_AIO: complete open-source ClientOpsCRM source plus the preserved Relay subsystem, build/run instructions, license, examples, and tests.
- DOCUMENTATION: final PDFs, matching LaTeX sources, and Markdown technical documents.
- RESEARCH: 100+ source register plus research synthesis and claim-to-design mapping.
- VERIFICATION: machine-readable test report, command transcript, checksums, archive inventory, and adversarial results.

The master archive must also contain a README_FIRST, release notes, file inventory, and SHA-256 manifest. No transient databases, compiled-object caches, temporary files, zero-byte files, or unverified claims may be included.
